a reading room sample

This is where a full article, PDF, or guide would open. The prototype centres the feeling of returning: one calm surface, your place held without ceremony.

Reading lists are often designed as funnels: add, save, optimise, complete. This one wants to be a shelf instead. Things may wait. They may travel between rooms. They may gather a note or two at the edge.

The real product would use a proper article reader and PDF.js, while storing the last scroll anchor or PDF page locally. Nothing leaves your device by default.
